Checking the server…

1. Log in to your services

Opens the server's browser. Log in to Peacock, Tubi and Sling once; the logins are saved.

Open the browser view

2. Put the channel on your Roku

  1. Turn on developer mode: on the Roku remote press Home ×3, Up ×2, Right, Left, Right, Left, Right, note the address it shows, choose Enable installer and restart, and set a password.
  2. Download the channel (the server's address is built in): Download Roku channel
  3. On a computer, open the address the Roku showed (e.g. http://192.168.1.50), log in as rokudev with your password, choose the zip, and press Install.
